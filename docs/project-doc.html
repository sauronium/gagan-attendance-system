Attendance, Field Visit & Client Messaging System

One web app, installable on any phone, that tracks who came to work and when, logs every field sales and service visit on a map, manages leave and staff records, keeps every client and AMC contract in one place, and sends the right SMS to the right client at the right time.

01

Overview

Agam Water runs an office team, a field sales team and AMC service technicians, and serves a growing list of clients with installed RO plants. Today attendance, leave, client records and service reminders live in different places, often on paper or in personal phones. This project brings them into three connected modules on one platform:

A · Attendance & employee management

Staff records, selfie + GPS check-in, live headcount, shifts and late rules, leave balances and approvals, payroll-ready monthly reports.

B · Field visits & visit map

Sales agents and technicians log each client stop with GPS, time and photo. Admins see the day's route on a map and verify or reject each visit.

C · Client management & messaging

Client and site directory, installed plants, AMC contracts and service schedules, technician assignment, and automatic SMS reminders (demo SMS API in this phase).

04

Architecture

A single Next.js application serves both the mobile staff app and the admin portal. MySQL holds all records, Cloudflare R2 stores check-in and visit photos, and OpenStreetMap draws the visit map. SMS go to a built-in demo SMS API for now. A scheduled job runs daily to work out which reminders are due.

Employee & attendance management

5.1 Employee records

Each employee has one profile that every other feature reads from: attendance rules come from their shift, leave quota from their leave policy, and approvals go to their reporting manager.

Profile

  • Employee code, name, photo
  • Mobile (login), email
  • Department & designation
  • Date of joining, date of birth
  • Emergency contact, address

Work setup

  • Role (see §03)
  • Assigned shift & weekly off
  • Work type: office / field / both
  • Allowed check-in location(s)
  • Reporting manager

Status & documents

  • Active · On notice · Inactive
  • Leave policy & opening balances
  • ID / documents upload (optional)
  • Monthly salary basis for exports
  • Full change history
Leaving staff are marked Inactive, never deleted, so their past attendance stays in reports and their login stops working the same day.

5.4 Corrections & regularisation

Employee requests

Missed check-out, wrong status or on-duty outside office (client meeting, training). The employee gives a time and reason; the manager approves or rejects; the day is recalculated.

Admin edits

Admins can override any day, but must enter a reason. The original value, new value, who and when are kept in the audit log and shown on the record.

  • Total active staff, present now, late, on leave, absent, not yet checked in
  • Split by department and by office / field
  • Tap any number to see the names and their check-in photos
  • Flagged check-ins (weak GPS, outside office) in a review queue

Reports & exports (CSV / Excel)

  • Monthly muster roll: one row per employee, one column per day
  • Payroll summary: paid days, loss-of-pay days, lates, overtime hours
  • Late-comers and early-leavers report
  • Leave register and balance statement
  • Employee directory and headcount trend

Field sales & service visits

Sales agents and AMC technicians spend most of the day at client sites. They log each stop from their phone when they arrive. Admins then see every stop on a map and mark it Verified or Rejected with a remark.

7.1 Logging a visit

01Arrive on siteField staff are already checked in for the day.
02Tap "Log visit"One button on the staff app home screen.
03Pick the clientSearch existing clients, or add a new prospect.
04GPS + server timePin, accuracy and time captured automatically.
05Details & photoPurpose, person met, notes, outcome, optional site photo.
06Sent for reviewAppears as Pending in the admin queue and map.

What a visit records

  • Client or new prospect name
  • Purpose: sales pitch, demo, follow-up, AMC service, complaint, payment collection, installation
  • Person met and their phone
  • Notes and an optional photo (watermarked like check-in selfies)
  • GPS pin, accuracy and server time

Outcome & follow-up added

  • Outcome: interested, quotation requested, order won, not interested, revisit
  • Follow-up date creates a reminder for the agent
  • New prospects can be turned into clients in one click
  • Full visit history shows on the client's timeline

Automatic checks added

  • Flagged if logged more than 500 m from the client's saved site pin
  • Flagged if GPS accuracy is weak
  • A technician's visit against a due AMC service marks that service done
  • Visits can't be logged while the employee is checked out

7.2 Admin visit map & verification queue

The map shows every logged stop as a pin coloured by status. Filtering to one agent and one day draws their route in time order, so gaps and long detours are easy to spot. Clicking a pin opens the visit with its photo, and the admin verifies or rejects it right there.

Client & AMC management

A client is the company or person who pays. A client can have several sites, each site has one or more installed plants, and each plant can be under an AMC contract that creates service visits. Every message sent to the client is stored on the same record.

Fig 6 Client record structure. A contract automatically creates its service visits.

8.1 Features

Client directory

  • Company / individual, GST number, segment (industrial, commercial, domestic, institution)
  • Multiple contacts, each with a phone, role and SMS opt-in
  • Multiple sites with address, area / city and map pin
  • Tags, lead source and assigned sales person
  • Search by name, phone, area or plant serial; Excel import for existing clients

Installed plants

  • Plant type, capacity (LPH), model, serial number
  • Membrane and filter specs, installation date, installed by
  • Warranty end date, with its own expiry reminder
  • Full service and parts-replacement history per plant

AMC contracts

  • Plan (comprehensive / labour-only), start & end date, contract value
  • Visits per year; the service schedule is generated from this
  • Filter / membrane replacement intervals
  • Payment status (paid, partial, due) and renewal status
  • One-click renew: copies terms to a new contract period

Service visits & technicians

  • Calendar of due visits by week and by technician
  • Assign or reassign a technician; both client and technician get an SMS
  • Technician marks visit done in the app: notes, parts used, photo
  • Status: Scheduled · Done · Missed · Rescheduled; next due date recalculated
  • Technician workload view so visits are spread evenly

Complaints / service requests added

  • Log a breakdown or complaint against a plant
  • Priority, assigned technician, target resolution time
  • Client gets a ticket number by SMS and an SMS when it's closed
  • Open / overdue complaints shown on the dashboard

Client timeline & reports

  • One timeline per client: contracts, visits, complaints, messages
  • AMC renewals due in 30 / 60 / 90 days
  • Expired, not renewed contracts (win-back list)
  • AMC revenue by month, visits done vs missed, technician performance
09

Client messaging (demo SMS API)

The full messaging system is built in this phase: templates, automatic reminders, manual and bulk sends, scheduling and logs. The only difference from a live setup is the last hop. Instead of a paid SMS gateway, messages go to a built-in demo SMS API that behaves like one and shows every message in an in-app inbox. Nothing reaches a real phone and nothing is charged.

How the demo SMS API works

  • Same request shape as a real gateway: number, sender, template and variables in; a message ID out
  • Returns sent at once, then delivered a few seconds later, like a real delivery report
  • A "simulate failures" switch marks a share of messages failed to test retries
  • The demo inbox shows each message as the client's phone would, grouped by phone number
  • The provider sits behind one adapter, so a live gateway later is a settings change, not a rewrite
Going live later (not in this phase): pick an SMS provider, register the sender ID and templates on India's DLT portal (2–7 working days), add the API key, switch the adapter from demo to live.

Integrity & security

Attendance & visits can't be faked easily

  • Time always comes from the server, never the phone
  • Live camera only; gallery uploads blocked
  • Time and location burned into the photo before upload
  • GPS accuracy checked; weak or far-away locks flagged
  • Visits far from the client's saved site pin flagged for review
  • One active device per employee; new device needs admin OK

Access & accountability

  • Mobile number + password, or mobile OTP login
  • Role checked on every request on the server, not only hidden in the UI
  • Every manual change logged with who, when, before, after and why
  • Session timeout on shared office computers

Data protection

  • HTTPS everywhere; photos in a private R2 bucket with expiring links
  • Daily database backup, kept 30 days
  • Check-in photos auto-deleted after 12 months (setting)
  • Client phone numbers visible only to roles that need them

SMS demo safety

  • Demo API never contacts a real phone network
  • Every screen and log entry clearly marked DEMO
  • Opt-out, quiet hours and rate limits already enforced, so going live changes no behaviour
  • Only approved templates can be sent, same as live
